To change to between compressed sensing and naive proportional rescaling:
Go to line 203 and 204 of test_multiDataLoader.py and uncomment line
Use "ratio = rescale_ratio(sampled_pts, pred_init,relative_C=0.05)" for compressed sensing, tune regulariser with change
Use "ratio = rescale_ratio_proportional(sampled_pts, pred_init)" for naive proportional

Run compressed_sensing.py for sanity check.
